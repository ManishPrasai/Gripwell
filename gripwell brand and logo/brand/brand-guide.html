Gripwell · Brand guide v1.0

Portfolio demo brand

The G's crossbar has broken away to become a lane dash. Every cut in the mark is horizontal, like markings on a road, and the dash is exactly as thick as the stroke of the G. Precise, confident, modern.

Clear space

x
x

Keep at least x clear on every side, where x is the height of the lane dash. Nothing else (text, edges, images) should come inside this space.

Use orange sparingly: the lane dash, plus one small accent per screen at most. Text on orange is always #16181D (6.2:1); white on orange fails at 2.9:1. Graphite on white is 13.6:1. Never use orange for text on white or warm white.

Logo versions

Colour on light backgrounds, graphite one-colour for single-colour print, reversed (warm white with orange dash) on dark, all-white on graphite or photos.

Typography

Manrope

Wordmark: Manrope Bold, lowercase, tracking −0.05 em, converted to outlines. Use Manrope (SIL OFL) for UI text: 700 for headings, 500 for body.

Don't

✕Recolour the dash or the G
✕Join the dash to the stem
✕Stretch or squash the mark
✕Italicise or slant it
✕
Put white on orange (use #16181D)
✕Add shadows, gradients or outlines
