What a tyre fitting includes

You choose the tyres, the branch and the day, and the bay is held for you. Fitting is priced per tyre and shown for each branch before you book. Every wheel is balanced and gets a new valve, and the old tyres are taken away. Warranty claims start online, with the form and your photos.
