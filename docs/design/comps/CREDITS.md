CARTO Positron was tested first; it now returns an "API KEY REQUIRED" tile without a key.

Tyre image `tyre-passenger.png` and the logo files are copied from `apps/customer/public`. `tyre-three-quarter-600.png` is a resized copy; Comp F used it for all four deals until round 5 (2026-11-20), when the deal cards became typographic (size-led) because one photo four times read as a placeholder.
Rejected for F: a Shixart1985 balancing-machine photo (large PROVAC logo), oil-check shots showing VW badges, and a bonnet-up shot that was out of focus and showed a number plate.

The comps are generated by `build_comps.py` (run from the repo root: `python docs/design/comps/build_comps.py docs/design/comps f`; name comps to rebuild only those, A–D are signed off; E has its own build).
Comp F revision (2026-11-20, after the 26/40 critique): branch photos removed (they showed residential streets, not branches), and the Tyre fitting jack shot and Puncture repair tread close-up dropped. Commons searches for a bright, logo-free tyre-machine or puncture-plug photo found nothing usable (Dell/Twin Busch/Slime logos, bicycle kits, dark garages).
